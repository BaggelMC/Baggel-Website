Each claim has a whitelist. The whitelist lets you define which players can bypass your claim's protections. Players in the whitelist can do anything on the claim they are whitelisted on. Whitelisted players are not able to manage the claims they are only whitelisted in.

You can manage the whitelist via the [Claim Managemnet UI](../claims#claim-management-ui) or via commands:

`/claim whitelist <add/remove> <type> <claim name> <player>`
