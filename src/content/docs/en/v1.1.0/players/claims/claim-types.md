There are different types of claims. The different types change who the claim belongs to.

## Player Claims
Player claims only belong to one individual player. No other player can modify that particular claim. By default Players have a claim limit and can only own up to **256** chunks.

## Team Claims
Team claims belong to an entire team and can be managed by everyone in that team. By default Teams have a claim limit and can only own up to **1024** chunks.

## Server Claims
Server claims are claims reserved for the server administrators to protect areas such as the server spawn.

## Placeholder Claims
Placeholder claims are a special type of claim reserved for the server administrators. They do not have protections. Their purpose is to prevent players from creating claims on that specific area, but do not affect the world or player capabilities in any way.
