The `/spawn` command allows players to teleport to the world's spawn location.

## Usage

```text
/spawn
```

When executed, a teleport countdown begins. Once the countdown has completed, the player will be teleported to spawn.

### Teleport Delay

To prevent players from escaping combat or dangerous situations instantly, players may be required to stand still before the teleport is performed.

Any movement input during the countdown will cancel the teleport.

The length of the countdown is determined by the server configuration and may vary between servers.

## Why Does This Exist?

Players can occasionally become trapped in locations from which they cannot reasonably escape, such as inside protected claims or inaccessible terrain.

The `/spawn` command provides a reliable way to return to a safe location without requiring administrative assistance.
