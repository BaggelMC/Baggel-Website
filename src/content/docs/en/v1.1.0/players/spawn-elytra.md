The **Spawn Elytra** system gives players temporary elytra flight inside a defined **elytra zone**. An area set up by the server administrators, usually around spawn or the starting island. The feature helps players **comfortably spread out across a larger area** at the beginning of a season or event, without needing to first earn their own elytra.

This system was inspired by the **Craft Attack** series and is designed to make the early-game experience more dynamic. It allows everyone to spread out from the starting area without granting long-term flight advantages.

# How It Works

Inside the elytra zone, you can **activate flight by double jumping**, just like with a regular elytra. Once in the air, the spawn elytra behaves almost exactly like a normal one.

![Boost Key](/assets/images/spawn-elytra/boost-key.png)

While gliding, you can press the **Swap Item With Off-Hand** key (`F` by default) to receive a one-time boost. This helps you gain a bit of altitude or reach distant areas more easily.

The spawn elytra can only be activated inside the elytra zone. Once you’ve taken off, you can glide beyond the zone’s boundaries until you land. However, you cannot start flying again unless you re-enter the defined zone.

# Stuck

Despite improved landing rules, it is still possible for players to occasionally get stuck in the flying state after landing.

![Stuck Example](/assets/images/spawn-elytra/stuck-example.png)

While it is unlikely, in the rare event that it does happen, **logging out and back in** will always get you out of the flying state. That's why it is important to **never carelessly log out mid-flight**, or gravity might take over.

# Restrictions.

The spawn elytra is only available in **Survival** and **Adventure** modes.

**Firework rockets** can by default not be used with the spawn elytra.

If you have an actual elytra equipped, using the spawn elytra will **drain durability** from it.
